# Raw Outputs

This folder contains the machine-readable outputs generated by the promo discovery pipeline for 50 screenplay episodes.

## Folder contents

### 01_episode_analysis
- `episode_analysis.json`: Episode-level narrative summaries, story developments, unresolved storylines and discovered promo moments for all 50 episodes.

### 02_candidate_discovery
- `discovered_candidates.json`: 64 promo candidates identified during episode analysis.
- `shortlist.json`: 30 candidates selected for boundary optimization and evaluation.

### 03_promo_recommendations
- `ranked_promos.json`: Complete ranked output for the 30 shortlisted candidates, including source segments, timestamps, evaluation scores and recommendations.
- `accepted_promos.json`: Candidates recommended for acceptance.
- `review_promos.json`: Candidates retained for human editorial review.
- `rejected_promos.json`: Candidate rejected by the evaluation stage.

### 04_reports
- `discovery_stats.json`: Pipeline execution statistics, including episode counts, candidate counts, API usage, boundary optimization, evaluation and deduplication results.

## Output notes and limitations

- The accepted output contains 16 valid accepted promo records and two additional malformed entries without candidate identifiers or recommendation fields. These two entries are retained in the original output and should not be treated as promo recommendations.
- One candidate from Episode 24 could not complete promo evaluation and was retained for human review.
- Evaluation scores and acceptance thresholds are prototype-level decision aids, not measured audience-performance results or official Kuku FM benchmarks.

All timestamps and source segment references are derived from the parsed screenplay data.
